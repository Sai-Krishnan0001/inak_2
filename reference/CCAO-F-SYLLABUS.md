**Not a case-study exam.** The guide describes discrete items written against objectives. There
is no scenario-block structure of the kind that CCAR-F turned out to use. Standalone items are
the right shape to practise.

**Criterion-referenced.** Section 9: you are measured against a fixed standard set by a formal
standard-setting study, not against other candidates. Section-level percentages are reported but
**are not used to determine pass/fail** — only the total scaled score is.

### The 30 objectives — items are written against these

**D1 Prompting and Task Execution (14%)**
1. Create effective prompts for business and technical tasks
2. Apply task decomposition techniques to structure complex requests
3. Iterate prompts to improve output quality
4. Adapt prompting strategies based on task type (analysis, research, drafting, brainstorming)

**D2 Output Evaluation and Validation (21%)**
1. Evaluate Claude-generated outputs for accuracy and completeness
2. Identify hallucinations, inconsistencies, and biases in responses
3. Apply fact-checking and validation techniques
4. Determine when human review or additional verification is required
5. Edit, adapt, refine, and compare outputs for the intended audience
6. Organize and curate information and select appropriate output formats (artifacts, inline, structured data)

**D3 Product and Model Selection (12%)**
1. Select appropriate Claude product features (Projects, research mode, chat, artifacts)
2. Differentiate between Claude model types (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus)
3. Align model selection with task requirements (cost, speed, quality)
4. Understand and manage context limitations and memory considerations (when to restart, summarize, or persist)

**D4 Workflow Integration and Solution Design (16%)**
1. Apply Claude to analyze requirements and use cases
2. Leverage Claude for research, planning, and process optimization
3. Use Claude to support solution design, development, and iteration
4. Integrate Claude into existing workflows to augment or redesign them
5. Communicate Claude's value and limitations to stakeholders

**D5 Configuration and Knowledge Management (12%)**
1. Configure Claude Projects with instructions and knowledge sources
2. Manage uploaded knowledge and **connectors (e.g., Google Drive, Gmail)**
3. Create effective system-level instructions
4. Inform, maintain, and update Claude configurations, knowledge sources, and instructions

**D6 Governance, Risk, and Responsible Use (15%)**
1. Identify appropriate and inappropriate use cases
2. Apply data sensitivity, regulatory, and privacy considerations
3. Follow organizational AI policies and governance standards
4. Understand the ethical implications of AI usage

**D7 Troubleshooting and Optimization (10%)**
1. Identify, diagnose, and resolve issues with underperforming prompts or poor outputs
2. Adjust approach based on feedback and results
3. Optimize workflows for efficiency and effectiveness

## Audience and prerequisites — Sections 3 and 4

For professionals who use Claude as a productivity tool and build Claude Projects: operations,
marketing, project management, education, communications, general knowledge work. Explicitly
**not** for software developers building against APIs or designing agentic systems — that is the
Architect and Developer scope, to which Associates *escalate* complex work.

**Prerequisites: none.** No mandatory courses, no software-development or API experience needed.
The credential is awarded on exam performance alone.

Recommended (not required): regular hands-on Claude use in a professional setting; foundational
understanding of structured problem-solving and workflow design; roles such as business analyst,
project manager, operations lead, marketing/comms/HR/education professional, consultant, or
knowledge worker; practical understanding of AI limitations including hallucinations, context
constraints and data sensitivity.

## Policies — Sections 10–14

- **Registration:** Anthropic Partner Academy → checkout → Pearson VUE account → schedule.
  Cancel or reschedule up to **24 hours** before; inside 24 hours forfeits the fee.
- **ID:** valid, unexpired, government-issued photo ID; name must match registration exactly.
  Name corrections via `certifications-support@anthropic.com` **before** scheduling.
- **Retakes:** **14 days** after the first failure, **30** after the second, **90** after the
  third. Maximum **four attempts per rolling twelve months**, per exam. Fee applies each time.
- **Accommodations:** requested and approved through Pearson VUE **before** scheduling.
- **NDA:** you must accept a confidentiality agreement or the session ends with no refund. All
  exam content is Anthropic's proprietary property.
- **Renewal:** 12 months. On-time renewal is a **free, non-proctored assessment** on the Partner
  Academy. A lapsed credential means retaking the full exam at full fee.
- **Tier eligibility:** per the certifications page, the Associate credential **does not count
  toward Claude Partner Network tier eligibility**.
